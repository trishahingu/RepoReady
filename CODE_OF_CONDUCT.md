
# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming and respectful environment for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and inclusive.
- Welcome constructive feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the community.
- Treat all contributors with kindness.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment or discrimination.
- Personal attacks or insults.
- Trolling or disruptive behavior.
- Publishing private information without permission.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to the repository maintainer through GitHub.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all RepoReady community spaces.

Thank you for helping keep RepoReady welcoming!
